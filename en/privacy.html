Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Fully offline No data collected No account required

This Privacy Policy explains how the InterestMe mobile application (“the App”), published by DevOP, handles your information. It applies to version 1.0.0 of the App. The short version: the App is fully offline and does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. The details are below.

1Who we are

The App is published by DevOP. If you have any question about this policy, you can reach us at support@interestme.example.

2The short version

  • The App works completely offline. It does not connect to the internet on its own.
  • We do not collect, receive, or store any personal data on servers.
  • There is no account, no registration, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device.

3Data stored on your device

The App stores your input only on your own device, in a local database and local preferences. This may include:

  • your name, if you choose to enter it when you first open the App;
  • your saved compound interest simulations and savings goal plans;
  • your preferences (currency, number format, appearance).

This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted with it.

4Information you send us voluntarily

If you use the feedback feature, or email us directly at support@interestme.example, you may send us your email address and the content of your message. The App only opens your device's email application; nothing is sent to us unless you actually send the email.

We use the information you send only to respond to you. We do not add you to mailing lists or use it for marketing. We keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

5No advertising, analytics or tracking

We do not use advertising, analytics services, crash-reporting tools, or tracking of any kind. The App contains no third-party trackers or SDKs.

6Third-party links

The App may open links in your browser (for example, these pages) or your device's email application. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they process your data. You should review their policies before using them.

7Children

The App is not directed at children. Because the App does not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children either.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data that relates to you. Because the App itself holds no personal data about you, exercising these rights typically means deleting the App and its local data on your device. For anything related to the messages you have sent us, contact us at support@interestme.example.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective date” above and post the new version on this page.

10Contact

Questions or requests: support@interestme.example
